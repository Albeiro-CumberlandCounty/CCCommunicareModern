# Cumberland County Privacy Source for CommuniCare Terms/Privacy Rewrite

Sarah Hallock requested that the modern CommuniCare website's Terms/Privacy language mirror Cumberland County's approach.

Current public County source identified during M3 review:

- Cumberland County Privacy Policy:
  https://www.cumberlandcountync.gov/departments/no-department-pages-group/privacy-policy

Implementation rule:

Do not copy the County policy verbatim. The County policy covers County-specific services and applications that are outside CommuniCare's static-site scope. Use its structure and principles as a model, then write CommuniCare-specific language that accurately describes:

- the static public website
- Google Analytics 4 and related cookies/usage analytics
- external links
- information voluntarily sent through email/phone
- the fact that the initial website does not collect sensitive information through a web form
- contact information for privacy questions

Final language remains subject to CommuniCare/ITS approval.
